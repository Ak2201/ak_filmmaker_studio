A studio for Tamil and Indian filmmakers, from idea to call sheet

{{brand:name}} is a working desk for screenwriting, pre-production and craft study, built to run in your browser and keep your work on your own device first.

Get started See plans

Why it exists

A film moves through many hands and many files before the first shoot day: a story, a screenplay, a breakdown, a schedule, a budget, a call sheet. We built one place where each of those reads the same scenes, so a change in the script reaches the plan.

It starts from how films here are made: dialogue in Tamil, Tanglish or English, a budget in rupees that starts from dated Chennai rates, and a reference library of films, directors and the rules of the craft.

Who it is for

Independent and first-time filmmakers, screenwriters and small crews: indie directors, writers and line producers who want the script and the plan in one place.

    It works offlineThe studio installs like an app and keeps working without a connection, on a film set included.

    Sync and backup when you want themYou enter the studio by signing in with Google. Syncing your films between devices comes with a studio membership, and a backup to your own Google Drive is there if you switch it on.

    Nothing you write is deletedIf a part of the studio is closed to your plan, what you wrote in it stays and reappears when your plan includes it. You can download all of your work at any time.
