Also in the studio

These are things the studio does that the table does not compare, because we have not checked the other product's side.

translate

Tamil typing in dialogue

Type Tamil in Roman letters and it is offered in Tamil script, inside dialogue and parentheticals only, so slug lines and action stay in English and the page count stays exact.

fact_check

Script to call sheet in one chain

Scene headings become scenes; the breakdown, stripboard, day out of days and call sheets all read those same scenes.

payments

A budget in rupees

A line-by-line estimate that starts from dated Chennai rate bands, takes its days from your schedule and tracks spending against the plan.

backup

Backup to your own Google Drive

On Intermediate and Pro, a backup file of your studio is written to your own Drive, which you can open, download or delete.
