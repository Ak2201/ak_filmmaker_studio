## 4. What could not be tested live, and the owner steps

The sandbox cannot reach real AI providers, live Razorpay, real Supabase, Google
OAuth or Drive, a real microphone or voices, printers, or a real service worker
on the production host. Every proof ran against the fakes in `scripts/`. Please
do these yourself on the live site before selling:

1. **AI against a real key (Anthropic and Gemini).** Run Break down everything on
   preprod.html#drafts (Dragon sample, Pro), then Accept all, Undo, Reject and a
   Stop mid-run. Check the "about N requests" figure against what your provider
   console shows (r4-K-3). Try Enhance with AI, Draft arcs, Audience test, the
   blocking draft and enhance, and the storyboard frames (Gemini plus Drive).
   Look for real 529 and 500 behaviour: the busy-model retry was only proved
   against a fake.
2. **Sign-in, sync and co-editing with two real accounts.** Google sign-in, the
   account tour, Drive backup and restore, Drive-only storage, cloud sync, a
   project deleted on one device then checked on another (do its IndexedDB AI
   drafts go? only the hub and Erase paths were fixed, r4-H-2), the two-window
   Writer fix (r6-B-1) across two real devices, and co-editing merges.
3. **Billing.** A real Razorpay purchase (test, then live), the receipt e-mail
   (Resend is still unset, see CLAUDE.md: do not take a real payment before it
   is), refund, plan stacking and the pass offer. `prove:billing` only ran a fake.
4. **The gated production build.** `prove:gate` refuses the open build, so the
   site gate, device lock and admin console proofs ran against the gated `dist/`
   in the last gate (section 6). Click through the real gated site signed out and
   signed in once.
5. **Real devices.** Phone toolbar widths 320-719px on a real phone (r5-B-1),
   touch drag on the Beat Sheet and blocking map, pinch zoom on the map, the
   real keyboard and a screen reader through the Draft space and the Writer.
6. **Print and PDF.** Screenplay, sides, call sheet, DPR, top sheet, settlement,
   change report, A-pages and revision PDFs, the receipt, and the Studio overview.
   `window.print` was stubbed everywhere, so no printed page was seen.
7. **Dictation, read-aloud voices, geolocation on the call sheet, Maps and
   WhatsApp links.**
8. **The service worker** on the real host with an older worker upgrading in
   place, and offline use.
9. **Real script files.** Import real `.docx`, `.fdx`, `.fountain` and scanned or
   encrypted PDFs, and a 120-page script through the lock and extract.
10. **Short-film and fresh-project end-to-end walks** by hand, through the
    Stripboard, call sheets and budget and back, in Tamil as well as English.
11. **`prove:storage` and `prove:drive`** fail only on the sandbox proxy
    (`net::ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED`, same failures on `origin/develop`
    before these rounds). Run them on a machine with normal network access.
