# FilmMakerStudio — brief for video makers and content creators

Hand this file to anyone making a video, reel, ad or post about the studio.
It covers what the product is, every feature worth showing, the plans and
prices, the messages to land, ten ready video concepts, hooks in English and
Tamil, how to record the screens, and the claims you must **not** make.

Everything here is true of the product as of 5 Nov 2026. Where a figure
changes day to day (offer price, deadline, places left) it is a
`{PLACEHOLDER}`: ask the owner for that day's figure before you publish.

---

## 1. The product in one breath

**One line:** Write, plan and shoot your film — in one studio, in your browser.

**30 words:** FilmMakerStudio takes one film from idea to screenplay to
breakdown, schedule, budget, call sheets and the edit. Every tool reads the
same scenes, so nothing is typed twice. Built for Indian and Tamil crews.

**The core promise:** a scene you write once becomes the breakdown, the shot
list, the stripboard, the call sheet and the budget line. No spreadsheets,
no copying between five apps.

**Who it is for**

| Audience | What they want to hear |
| --- | --- |
| Independent and short-film makers | One tool from the first idea to the shoot day, at the price of a tea a day |
| Screenwriters | A proper screenplay writer that formats itself, in Tamil, Tanglish or English |
| Assistant directors and line producers | Breakdown, stripboard, call sheets and budget from the script, not typed again |
| Film and Viscom students | A real production tool for the diploma film, at a student price |
| Buyers outside India (second) | The same studio, priced in dollars at checkout |

**Site:** https://filmmakerstudio.in/start · **Support:** support@filmmakerstudio.in

   forward become one place where every tool reads the same scenes.

## 6. Ten video concepts

Each concept lists its length, hook, beats with the exact screens, and CTA.
VO = voice-over; OST = on-screen text.

### 1. "One line to a call sheet" — 60s (hero ad)
- **Hook (0–3s):** OST "From one line… to a call sheet." Typing a logline.
- **Beats:** Story idea → Beat Sheet (drag a beat) → Screenplay typing a scene → one-click breakdown (scenes appear) → stripboard (drag scene to Day 2) → call sheet for Day 2 → "Send on WhatsApp".
- **VO:** "Write it once. Every scene becomes the breakdown, the schedule and the call sheet. No spreadsheets."
- **CTA:** "A year from about ₹1.64 a day. Continue with Google — filmmakerstudio.in"

### 2. "Preethi says…" — 15s (reel, writers)
- **Hook:** OST "Watch this line format itself."
- **Beats:** Type `Preethi says, "Where were you?"` → Return → it becomes PREETHI + dialogue. Then type `cut to:` → it jumps right as `CUT TO:`.
- **OST end:** "A screenplay writer that formats as you type. Tamil, Tanglish, English."

### 3. "Break down everything" — 30s (Pro, AI)
- **Hook:** OST "Your whole pre-production. One press."
- **Beats:** Pre-production page → press **Break down everything** → drafts arrive in parallel (shots, notes, budget, blocking) → open one → **Accept** → show Undo.
- **VO:** "The AI drafts. You decide. Nothing touches your film until you press Accept."
- **Note:** Say it uses your own AI key. Never call it free AI.

### 4. "Shoot day on your phone" — 30s (on-set, vertical)
- **Hook:** A real set at dawn. OST "Call sheet's ready before the tea is."
- **Beats:** A phone shows Shoot Day → tick a scene "Shot", another "Part" → airplane mode on → still working → that evening, the DPR.
- **VO:** "Built for the floor. Works without a signal."

### 5. "Less than a tea a day" — 15s (price)
- **Hook:** A cup of tea on a table. OST "₹1.64 a day."
- **Beats:** Quick cuts of screenplay → stripboard → budget → call sheet. OST "A whole year of the studio from about ₹1.64 a day."
- **Rule:** Do not invent a "competitor stack costs ₹X" figure.

### 6. "Your script stays with you" — 30s (trust)
- **Beats:** A writer opens a laptop → OST "Saved in your browser first" → backup download → sync toggle off → AI draft → Accept / Undo.
- **VO:** "Your script, your device, your call."

### 7. "The diploma film" — 45s (students)
- **Hook:** OST "Your first film, planned like a pro's."
- **Beats:** Student opens the **Dragon sample** → Library shelf → writes a short (Short Film Five-Beat) → breakdown → shot list print → storyboard frame.
- **CTA:** Student price **only if the owner confirms the code is live**.

### 8. "Spreadsheet chaos → one studio" — 30s (before/after)
- **Before (desaturated):** five browser tabs, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp forward with an outdated call time, a scribbled script page.
- **After (violet):** the same day in the studio: stripboard → call sheet → WhatsApp.
- **OST:** "Stop retyping your film."

### 9. "Blocking it out" — 30s (directors, cinematographers)
- **Beats:** Scene floor plan → place actors and camera → draw the camera path and the 180° line → next shot gets its own diagram → link to storyboard frame.
- **VO:** "One floor plan per scene. One diagram per shot."

### 10. "Full tour" — 3–5 min (YouTube, long form)
1. 0:00 Hook: the 60s ad cut-down.
2. 0:20 Open the Dragon sample.
3. 0:40 Story path and Beat Sheet (Lanes / Spine).
4. 1:20 Screenplay Writer: auto-format, Tamil line, read aloud, drafts and revision colours, lock.
5. 2:10 Breakdown → elements → shot list → storyboard → blocking.
6. 3:00 Stripboard, DOOD, budget from Chennai rates.
7. 3:40 Call sheet → WhatsApp → shoot-day phone view.
8. 4:10 Edit log and deliverables.

## 8. Recording guide

- **Use the sample feature:** open `https://filmmakerstudio.in/?sample=1`
  after signing in. The Dragon sample has scenes, cast, a schedule and call
  sheets already filled, so every screen in §2 looks real. It is a
  reconstruction for demos. Do not present it as a real production or put
  real people's names on it.
- **Desktop:** record at 1440×900 (or 1920×1080), browser zoom 100%, no
  bookmarks bar, signed in with a demo Google account and no personal email
  visible.
- **Phone:** record at 390px width (an iPhone 14 or similar), vertical, for
  Shoot Day, call sheets and the bottom bar.
- **Key pages and anchors:** `story.html`, `screenplay.html`,
  `screenplay.html#lock`, `drafts.html`, `breakdown.html`,
  `stripboard.html`, `budget.html`, `contacts.html`, `reports.html`,
  `shoot.html`, `edit.html`, `deliverables.html`, `preprod.html#drafts`
  (Break down everything).
- **Ready assets in the repo:** `social/poster-1..3.png` and
  `social/feature-01..15.png` (4:5 Instagram posters with true feature
  copy), `social/instagram-dp.png` (logo/profile), and the product-page
  clips made by `npm run record:marketing`.
- **AI shots:** these need a Pro account with an AI key in Settings → AI.
  Record a real run. Never fake output or speed it up without saying it is
  sped up.
- **Music and fonts:** use licensed music only. Match on-screen text to
  Gabarito or Onest where you can.

## 9. What you must NOT claim

These are binding. Breaking one is untrue, and some are unlawful under
India's consumer rules on dark patterns.

1. **No invented social proof.** No testimonials, user counts, "thousands of
   filmmakers", star ratings, awards or "as seen in" until the owner gives
   you a real, approved one.
2. **No invented scarcity.** No made-up countdowns or "only 3 left". Use a
   deadline or count only from the owner on the day of posting.
3. **No "free".** There is no free plan, and there is no public free trial
   while the launch pass is the offer. Do not say "free trial", "free
   forever" or "free AI".
4. **AI is Pro and uses your own key.** The AI provider bills the user
   directly. The audience test is always "simulated by a model, not real
   viewers".
5. **No competitor claims.** Do not name rivals, compare features or quote
   their prices.
6. **No promises beyond the product.** Do not claim it "replaces your AD",
   "guarantees festival selection", "makes your film for you", or offers
   "unlimited cloud storage".
7. **Refunds:** "Purchases are final, with three exceptions." Never "no
   refunds" and never "money-back guarantee".
8. **No personal names.** Never show or name the owner. Use demo accounts
   only.
9. **Switched-off tools stay off-screen:** Pitch Deck, Songs, Lookbook,
   Reference Media, Case Studies, Dissection, Glossary.
10. **Prices:** quote only ₹599 / ₹799 / ₹999 a year (₹1.64 / ₹2.19 / ₹2.74
    a day), or the live figures the owner gives you that day.

Send every final cut to the owner for a fact check before it goes live.
