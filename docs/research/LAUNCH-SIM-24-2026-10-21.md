# Simulated launch-reaction study: FilmMakerStudio (21 Oct 2026)

## What this is

This is a **simulation**. A language model played 24 personas and reacted to the public launch brief over two rounds: a first post, then replies to each other's posts. It is **not market research**. No real person saw the product. Personas reacted only to the brief, not to the app. Every count below is computed from the round 1 and round 2 data. Every quotation is copied verbatim from that data.

## Headline

- **Launch reaction: interested but waiting.** 14 of 24 were curious on first sight and 10 were skeptical. Only 1 (Chennai debut director) would try it "today". No persona said a firm "yes" to paying before the rise, in either round. In round 2, 15 said maybe and 9 said no, up from 4 no in round 1.
- **Objections are about trust, not price.** The biggest theme was a new, unproven tool from a small team that might shut down (11 of 24). Import/export fidelity came next (6), then privacy, sign-in and data control (5). Almost every persona called the price trivial.
- **The price rise did not create urgency.** Only 1 persona said it pushes them to buy now. 10 said it "feels like pressure, puts me off" and 13 said it had no effect. In round 2, 4 replies told others not to buy because of 1 Nov.
- **Segments.** Producers/directors/buyers (4 of 6 maybe) and hobbyists/creators (4 of 6 maybe) were the most open. Non-Tamil personas were 3 of 3 maybe, but only if their language works. Writers were the least likely: 3 of 5 final "no", and 2 of the 3 "would not recommend" in the whole panel. Crew were split 2 maybe, 2 no, mostly because they are not the buyers.
- **Most-wanted missing pieces:**
  - a stated trial length or a free/student/education tier (6)
  - privacy, offline and exit guarantees (4)
  - team accounts and roles (3)
  - editable rate cards or other currencies (3)
  - import from Excel, Celtx or an NLE (3)

Movement between rounds:
- **Pay before rise:** 5 moved from maybe to no (film student, 2nd AD, OTT writer, crowdfund producer, skeptic-dev). Nobody moved towards paying.
- **Try:** 2 moved from maybe to "yes, later" (line producer, skeptic-dev).
- **Self-reports don't match the answers:** 4 of the 5 who moved to "no" still marked their own change as "no change" (film student, 2nd AD, OTT writer, skeptic-dev). The 3 "more negative" were the Chennai debut director, the NRI filmmaker and the crowdfund producer.

What stands out:
- **Producers / directors / buyers:** all 3 "more negative" shifts came from this group. Their concerns were roles, data ownership and investor-ready exports.
- **Writers:** confidentiality decides it. The veteran and the assistant writer are both final no on paying and no on recommending.
- **Crew:** they don't control the purchase. The 2nd AD said "I'm crew, not the buyer". The editor said its value "depends on the producer and director using it".
- **Non-Tamil:** their interest depends on language support the brief does not confirm.
- **Hobbyists:** this is the only segment where everyone read the countdown as pressure.

## Word of mouth in round 2

There were 51 replies in total. 21 personas sent 2 each and 3 sent 3 each.

**Who drew the replies:**
- skeptic-dev: 9
- line-producer: 7
- chennai-debut: 6
- first-ad and exec-producer: 4 each

Seven personas got no replies: the YouTube maker, the tools influencer, the cinematographer, the editor, the NRI filmmaker, the assistant writer and the crowdfund producer. The thread grew around skeptic-dev's lock-out and exit questions and the line producer's question about who can see the budget.

**Advice to the Chennai debut director.** 5 of the 6 replies he received were cautions (veteran, bengaluru, influencer, nri, skeptic-dev). They told him to test exports, or keep his own copy, before trusting the tool. For example: "Export .fdx and Fountain on day one and keep them somewhere else." (Bengaluru indie)

**Advice not to buy because of the deadline.** 4 replies said so (veteran, bengaluru, influencer, skeptic-dev). For example: "Don't buy because of 1 Nov." (Tools influencer)

**Every persona said their questions went unanswered.** All 24 `whatChangedIt` answers say nobody had answered, tested or confirmed anything. For example: "Nobody from the company has answered." (Veteran writer)

**Shared needs pulled people together:**
- language: Malayalam with Telugu
- fdx fidelity: Celtx with OTT
- student and class pricing: film student with faculty
- budget roles: line producer, 2nd AD, NRI and crowdfund producer

## The price rise

| | Yes | Maybe | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay before rise (R1) | 0 | 20 | 4 |
| Pay after rise (R1) | 1 | 17 | 6 |
| Pay before rise (R2) | 0 | 15 | 9 |

**How answers moved from before the rise to after it (round 1):**
- Worse: 4 went from maybe to no (film student, media student, 2nd AD, Telugu writer).
- Better: 3 improved. The veteran writer and the film faculty went from no to maybe, and the NRI filmmaker went from maybe to yes ("Even Rs 1,999 is cheap.").

**Effect by reaction to the countdown:**
- **Put off (10):** final pay 5 no, 5 maybe. All 6 hobbyists/creators are in this group.
- **No effect (13):** final pay 4 no, 9 maybe.
- **Pushed to buy (1):** the Chennai debut director, final pay still maybe.

**Why it doesn't work:** the gap is small. The crowdfund producer said "the gap is only a few hundred rupees". The film faculty said "the deadline is shorter than our procurement takes".

## Caveats

- **These are not real people.** All 24 personas are played by one language model, so the agreement between them may be overstated. None of them used the product. They reacted only to the brief text.
- **Some objections may be about the brief, not the product.** Several objections say things like "the brief never says…". Some of those features may already exist and simply weren't described. This report does not check that.
- **The company never replied, by design.** No vendor was present in the simulated feed. So "nobody answered" (24 of 24) shows the setup of the simulation, not how responsive the real team is.
- **The round 1 plan was hypothetical.** It was asked even of people who said "no" to paying.
- **Mind-change labels don't always match the answers.** Four personas labelled themselves "no change" while their pay answer dropped from maybe to no.
- **Segment groups are my own.** The non-Tamil and "buyers" groups are small (3 and 6), so their percentages are fragile.
