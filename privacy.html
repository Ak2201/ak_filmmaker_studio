Privacy

What this app stores, where it stores it, and what never leaves your device.

Last updated 3 October 2026

The Filmmaker's Studio runs entirely in your browser. There is no server of ours behind it. Everything you write — scripts, scene breakdowns, budgets, contacts, notes — is saved in your own browser's local storage on the device you wrote it on, and stays there unless you deliberately turn on one of the optional features below.

We do not operate a database of your work, cannot read it, and have no way to recover it for you if you clear your browser data.

What the app stores on your device

Your work is held in your browser's localStorage under keys beginning fms_. This includes every project you create and everything in it, plus your preferences — which theme you chose, which project is open, and so on.

Browser storage is per-browser and per-device. Work saved in Chrome on a laptop is not visible in Safari on a phone, and clearing site data for this domain deletes it permanently. The Export button on the hub writes the whole studio to a file you keep; that file is the only backup the app has unless you connect one of the services below.

The app sets no cookies and runs no analytics, trackers, advertising or session-recording of any kind. Nothing measures what you do here.

Optional: syncing to your own Supabase project

You may connect an account so that projects follow you between devices and can be shared with collaborators. This uses Supabase, and the project it talks to is the one you configure by entering its URL and public ("anon") key into the app. Your work is written to that database, which belongs to whoever owns it — not to us.

Signing in is handled by Supabase Auth and Google is the only provider offered. When you sign in, Supabase stores the email address on your Google account so that projects, comments and share invitations can be attributed to you. Your Google password is never seen by this app or by Supabase.

If you never enter a Supabase URL and key, none of this happens and no account exists.

Optional: backing up to your own Google Drive

You may connect Google Drive to keep one backup file current. Three things about how that is built are worth stating plainly:

  • The permission requested is drive.file. It grants access only to files this app itself created. The app cannot see, list, read or alter anything else in your Drive — not your documents, not your photos, not files another app made.
  • It writes one file, named filmmakers-studio-backup.json, in your own Drive, where you can open, copy, download or delete it yourself. It contains the same thing the Export button produces. Disconnecting Drive from the app never deletes that file.
  • The access token is never written to disk. It is held in memory for the life of the browser tab and is gone when you close it. There is no refresh token and no client secret, because a static site has nowhere safe to keep either.

You can revoke the app's access to your Drive at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Optional: AI features with your own API key

Some tools — drafting a shot division, critiquing beats, turning a synopsis into a script — call a large language model. They are off unless you supply your own API key for Anthropic or Google Gemini.

That key is stored on your device only, under fms_ai_key_v1. It is sent as a request header directly from your browser to the provider the key belongs to, and never to us. It is deliberately excluded from exports and from the Drive backup, so a backup file you hand to somebody else does not carry your key. When you use these features, the text you send is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy, not this one.

Third parties your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — typefaces, on every page. Like any request to a third party, this reveals your IP address to Google.
  • Your Supabase project — only once you have configured one.
  • Google Identity Services and the Google Drive API — only once you connect Drive.
  • Anthropic or Google Gemini — only when you run an AI tool with your own key.

Nothing else is contacted. The site's Content-Security-Policy names this exact list, so a request to anywhere else is refused by your browser before it is sent.

Children

This is a professional filmmaking tool and is not directed at children under 13. No age information is collected, because no personal information is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because the app holds no contact details for you, there is no mailing list to notify — the current version is always the one on this page.
