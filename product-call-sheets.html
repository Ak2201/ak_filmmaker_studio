Built for the way a unit actually reads

A call sheet holds scene numbers and people, not copies of them. Rename a person or move a scene and every sheet says the new thing.

A unit often reads the production group on WhatsApp at five in the morning, not a PDF. So each sheet also becomes a plain-text message, for everyone or for one person with their own call time first.

  • WhatsAppSend to the whole unit or one person
  • OfflineSunrise and golden hour worked out on-device
