The list your AD and DOP both read

Each shot belongs to a scene and carries its number, size, angle, movement, lens and one line of what we see. Totals at the foot show setups, scenes and pages.

Slug lines and page lengths are read from the scene at print time, so a renamed location is renamed on the sheet without anyone editing it.
