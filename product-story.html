How it works Pricing

Structure you can see, not just read about

Pick a framework such as Three-Act, Save the Cat, Story Circle or the Short Film Five-Beat, and place your synopsis against its beats. Switch frameworks and your marked passages move to the nearest beat.

Tension is plotted against the shape each structure expects. Pacing flags point at the stretch of story they mean, so you fix the sag in the outline, not in the third draft.

  • 11Story structures to place your beats on
  • 4 to 15Beats per structure, Kishotenketsu to Save the Cat
  • DerivedPacing flags are computed, never typed in
