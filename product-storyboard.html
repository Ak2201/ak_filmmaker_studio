One shot, one frame, always in step

A frame belongs to a shot, and a shot belongs to a scene. Rename a location in the breakdown and the storyboard says the new name, because it was never typed here.

Frames hold a link to a picture or a written description, not the picture itself. That keeps your studio light and your work saved in your own browser.
