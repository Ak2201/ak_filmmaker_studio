Stay in the scene, not in the margins

Press Return and the next element is guessed from the line you just wrote. Tab and Shift+Tab walk from character to parenthetical to dialogue, so your hands stay on the keys and your head stays in the story.

Every scene heading you write becomes a scene in the studio. The breakdown, schedule and call sheet read from your script, so a rewrite shows up everywhere without being typed twice.
