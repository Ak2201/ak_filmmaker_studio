Refund & Cancellation Policy

Plans are bought a year at a time and never renew by themselves. Purchases are final, with the exceptions below.

Last updated 21 October 2026

A plan is one year for one payment. Nothing renews automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel and you are never charged again unless you choose to buy another year or upgrade.

Purchases are final and not refundable — except for a duplicate or erroneous charge, a payment for a plan that was never activated, and any refund the law of India requires. Use the free trial to try the studio before you buy.

What changed on 21 October 2026

  • A plan is now bought for one year. Nothing renews automatically, so there is still nothing to cancel; you renew by buying the plan again.
  • The three exceptions are unchanged.
  • Section 6: a refund is made in the currency you paid in, Indian Rupees or US dollars, and a conversion fee your bank or card issuer charged is between you and them.
  • Section 7: a refund takes off the year that payment bought; other years you paid for stay on your plan.
  • On 19 October 2026: the free trial became how the studio is tried before buying, section 5 added the refund request form and where to find your payment ID, and section 6 said we e-mail you when a refund is processed or fails.

1. Who sells the plans, and what this policy covers

{{brand:name}} is provided from India by a sole proprietor; it is a trading name, not a registered company. This policy applies to paid plans bought on {{brand:name}} and paid through Razorpay. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Cancellation

  • Paid plans are bought with a single payment for one year and do not renew by themselves, so there is no recurring charge to cancel. If you do not want another year, do nothing: the plan ends on the date your plan page shows.
  • If you start a payment and close the checkout before it completes, nothing is charged and no plan is activated.
  • You can stop using the Service, or ask us to delete your account, at any time. Neither entitles you to a refund outside the exceptions below.

3. Purchases are final

Because you can try the studio with a free trial and see what each plan includes before buying, a plan, once bought and activated, is not refundable: not for a change of mind, not for the unused part of a year, and not when you stop using the Service or delete your account. Renewals and upgrades are new purchases, of the price you paid for them, and are final in the same way.

4. The exceptions

These are not discretionary. If one applies, the refund is yours.

  • A duplicate or erroneous charge — if you were charged more than once for the same order, or charged an amount you did not agree to at checkout, the extra or erroneous payment is refunded in full.
  • Charged but not delivered — if your payment succeeded but the plan was never activated on your account, you choose: we activate the plan you paid for, or refund the payment in full.
  • Where the law requires it — wherever the law of India, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, requires a refund, it is given.

5. How to ask

E-mail {{brand:email}} from the e-mail address on your account, with the Razorpay payment or order ID and a line saying what went wrong. The payment ID is on the receipt we e-mailed you and on the invoice you can download from your plan page. We will reply within 2 working days.

Where we have switched it on, your plan page also has a Request a refund form: choose the reason that fits and add a note; a request is weighed against the exceptions above whichever reason you choose. A request there is the same as an e-mail; it does not by itself refund anything, and we answer it the same way.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method — card, UPI, net banking or wallet.
  • Once issued, a Razorpay refund reaches the original payment method in 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds are made in the currency you paid in (Indian Rupees or US dollars), for the amount you paid.
  • If your bank or card issuer converted the payment from another currency, it converts the refund back at its own rate on the day, so the amount that reaches you can differ from the amount that left your account. Any conversion or foreign-transaction fee it charged was its own, not ours, and is a matter between you and your bank.
  • We e-mail you when the refund has been processed, or if the payment provider could not complete it.

7. What happens to your account

When a refund is issued, the year that payment bought is taken off your plan; any other year you paid for stays. If no paid time is left, the plan ends and your account is given a fresh free trial, for the length the studio currently sets for its free trial (the app shows it). There is no free plan: when that trial ends, a plan is needed again whenever the studio requires one (section 3 of the Terms of Service). Projects saved on your devices are not affected. Synced projects beyond what the free trial allows stay on your devices but may stop syncing until you remove some or buy a plan again.

8. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, and disputes about it are subject to the courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, as set out in the Terms of Service, without affecting any right you have as a consumer.
