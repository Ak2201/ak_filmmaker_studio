Refund & Cancellation Policy

Plans are a one-time purchase. Purchases are final, with the exceptions below.

Last updated 19 October 2026

Plans are a one-time purchase. Nothing renews automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel and you will never be charged again unless you choose to upgrade.

Purchases are final and not refundable — except for a duplicate or erroneous charge, a payment for a plan that was never activated, and any refund the law of India requires. Use the free trial to try the studio before you buy.

What changed on 19 October 2026

  • The three exceptions are unchanged.
  • “Try every module free” is now “use the free trial”: the trial is how the studio is tried before buying.
  • Section 5 adds the refund request form on your plan page, where we have switched it on, and says where to find your payment ID (the receipt e-mail and the invoice).
  • Section 6 says we e-mail you when a refund is processed, or if it fails.

1. Who sells the plans, and what this policy covers

{{brand:name}} is provided from India by a sole proprietor; it is a trading name, not a registered company. This policy applies to paid plans bought on {{brand:name}} and paid through Razorpay. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Cancellation

  • Paid plans are bought with a single payment and do not renew, so there is no recurring charge to cancel.
  • If you start a payment and close the checkout before it completes, nothing is charged and no plan is activated.
  • You can stop using the Service, or ask us to delete your account, at any time. Neither entitles you to a refund outside the exceptions below.

3. Purchases are final

Because a free trial lets you try the studio before buying, a plan, once bought and activated, is not refundable: not for a change of mind, not for unused time, and not when you stop using the Service or delete your account. Upgrades are a new purchase of the price you paid for the upgrade and are final in the same way.

4. The exceptions

These are not discretionary. If one applies, the refund is yours.

  • A duplicate or erroneous charge — if you were charged more than once for the same order, or charged an amount you did not agree to at checkout, the extra or erroneous payment is refunded in full.
  • Charged but not delivered — if your payment succeeded but the plan was never activated on your account, you choose: we activate the plan you paid for, or refund the payment in full.
  • Where the law requires it — wherever the law of India, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, requires a refund, it is given.

5. How to ask

E-mail {{brand:email}} from the e-mail address on your account, with the Razorpay payment or order ID and a line saying what went wrong. The payment ID is on the receipt we e-mailed you and on the invoice you can download from your plan page. We will reply within 2 working days.

Where we have switched it on, your plan page also has a Request a refund form: choose which exception applies and add a note. A request there is the same as an e-mail; it does not by itself refund anything, and we answer it the same way.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method — card, UPI, net banking or wallet.
  • Once issued, a Razorpay refund reaches the original payment method in 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds are made in Indian Rupees, for the amount you paid.
  • We e-mail you when the refund has been processed, or if the payment provider could not complete it.

7. What happens to your account

When a refund is issued, the plan it paid for ends and your account is given a fresh free trial, for the length the studio currently sets for its free trial (the app shows it). There is no free plan: when that trial ends, a plan is needed again. Projects saved on your devices are not affected. Synced projects beyond what the free trial allows stay on your devices but may stop syncing until you remove some or buy a plan again.

8. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, and disputes about it are subject to the courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, as set out in the Terms of Service, without affecting any right you have as a consumer.
