Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds work for FilmMakerStudio plans, and what cancelling means.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Plans are a one-time purchase. Nothing renews automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel and you will never be charged again unless you choose to upgrade.

Changed your mind? You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to paid plans bought on FilmMakerStudio and paid through Razorpay. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Cancellation

  • Paid plans are bought with a single payment and do not renew, so there is no recurring charge to cancel.
  • If you start a payment and close the checkout before it completes, nothing is charged and no plan is activated.
  • You can stop using the Service, or ask us to delete your account, at any time. Deleting your account does not by itself entitle you to a refund outside the cases below.

3. When you can get a refund

  • Within 7 days of purchase — a full refund, for any reason, for your first purchase of a plan.
  • Duplicate or mistaken charges — if you were charged twice for the same plan, the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Payment taken but plan not activated — if your payment succeeded but your plan did not activate and we cannot fix it within 3 working days, you will receive a full refund.
  • Service discontinued — if we discontinue the Service, we will explain in advance what refunds, if any, are available.

Outside these cases, purchases are not refundable, except where Indian law requires otherwise. Upgrades are treated as a new purchase of the price you paid for the upgrade.

4. How to ask for a refund

E-mail arunaaron85@gmail.com from the e-mail address on your account, with the Razorpay payment or order ID (it is on the receipt Razorpay sent you) and a line saying what went wrong. We will reply within 2 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method — card, UPI, net banking or wallet.
  • We start the refund within 5 working days of approving it. Your bank or payment provider usually takes a further 5–7 working days to show it.
  • Refunds are made in Indian Rupees, for the amount you paid.

6. What happens to your account

When a refund is issued, the plan it paid for ends and your organisation returns to the free plan’s limits. Projects saved on your devices are not affected. Synced projects beyond the free plan’s limits stay on your devices but may stop syncing until you remove some or buy a plan again.

7. Contact

Questions about a payment or a refund: arunaaron85@gmail.com.
