Refund & Cancellation Policy

Plans are bought a year at a time. Purchases are final, with the exceptions below, and cancelling an auto-pay stops the charges to come.

A plan is one year at a time. A plan or a launch pass is sold either as a one-time purchase or on auto-renew, and which one is shown beside its price before you pay. Bought one-time, nothing renews and you are never charged again unless you choose to buy another year or upgrade. On auto-renew, each year (or each launch pass period) is its own charge; you can cancel anytime, which stops the charges still to come and does not refund the period already paid.

Purchases are final and not refundable — except for a duplicate or erroneous charge, a payment for a plan that was never activated, and any refund the law of India requires. While a launch offer runs, a launch pass lets you try a plan’s tools, for the number of days and at the price shown before you pay, before you buy a year.

1. Who sells the plans, and what this policy covers

{{brand:name}} is provided from India by a sole proprietor; it is a trading name, not a registered company. This policy applies to paid plans and launch passes bought on {{brand:name}} and paid through Razorpay. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Cancellation

  • Bought one-time. A plan or a pass bought one-time is a single payment for its period and does not renew, so there is no recurring charge to cancel. If you do not want another period, do nothing: it ends on the date your plan page shows.
  • On auto-renew. You can cancel anytime from the mandate itself: use the link in the Razorpay mandate e-mail, or cancel it in your bank or UPI app. The app does not cancel it. Cancelling stops every charge still to come. It does not refund the current period: your access continues to the end of the period already paid for, and then ends. Your work is kept either way.
  • If you start a payment and close the checkout before it completes, nothing is charged and no plan is activated.
  • You can stop using the Service, or ask us to delete your account, at any time. Neither entitles you to a refund outside the exceptions below.

3. Purchases are final

Because you can see what each plan includes before buying, and, while a launch offer runs, try a plan’s tools with a launch pass, a plan or a pass, once bought and activated, is not refundable: not for a change of mind, not for the unused part of a year, and not when you stop using the Service or delete your account. Renewals and upgrades are new purchases, of the price you paid for them, and are final in the same way.

4. The exceptions

These are not discretionary. If one applies, the refund is yours.

  • A duplicate or erroneous charge — if you were charged more than once for the same order, or charged an amount you did not agree to at checkout, the extra or erroneous payment is refunded in full.
  • Charged but not delivered — if your payment succeeded but the plan was never activated on your account, you choose: we activate the plan you paid for, or refund the payment in full.
  • Where the law requires it — wherever the law of India, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, requires a refund, it is given.

A launch pass is covered by these same three exceptions, and by nothing else.

On auto-renew, the three exceptions apply to each charge on its own: a duplicate or erroneous renewal charge, or a renewal charged where the period was never activated, is refunded as above. A charge taken after you cancelled an auto-pay, or after it had ended, adds nothing to your plan and is refunded in full. Cancelling an auto-pay is not one of the exceptions and does not refund the period already paid.

5. How to ask

E-mail {{brand:email}} from the e-mail address on your account, with the Razorpay payment or order ID and a line saying what went wrong. The payment ID is on the receipt we e-mailed you and on the invoice you can download from your plan page. We will reply within 2 working days.

Where we have switched it on, your plan page also has a Request a refund form: choose the reason that fits and add a note; a request is weighed against the exceptions above whichever reason you choose. A request there is the same as an e-mail; it does not by itself refund anything, and we answer it the same way.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method — card, UPI, net banking or wallet.
  • Once issued, a Razorpay refund reaches the original payment method in 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds are made in the currency you paid in (Indian Rupees or US dollars), for the amount you paid.
  • If your bank or card issuer converted the payment from another currency, it converts the refund back at its own rate on the day, so the amount that reaches you can differ from the amount that left your account. Any conversion or foreign-transaction fee it charged was its own, not ours, and is a matter between you and your bank.
  • We e-mail you when the refund has been processed, or if the payment provider could not complete it.

7. What happens to your account

When a refund is issued, the year that payment bought is taken off your plan; any other year you paid for stays, and on auto-renew the refund of one charge takes off only the period that charge bought. If no paid time is left, the plan ends and your account is given a fresh free trial, for the length the studio currently sets for its free trial (the app shows it). There is no free plan: when that trial ends, a plan is needed again whenever the studio requires one (section 3 of the Terms of Service). Projects saved on your devices are not affected. Synced projects beyond what the free trial allows stay on your devices but may stop syncing until you remove some or buy a plan again.

When a launch pass is refunded, the days it bought are taken off your account at once, and no free trial is given in their place. If you have since bought a yearly plan, which replaced the pass, the plan is not affected. If no paid time is left, the studio shows you the plans instead of your work whenever a plan is required, and your work is kept, not deleted.

8. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, and disputes about it are subject to the courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, as set out in the Terms of Service, without affecting any right you have as a consumer.
