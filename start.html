Questions before you buy

Is my script private?

Your work is saved in your own browser first. Sync to your other devices is optional, and yours to switch off. Read the Privacy Policy

Does it work offline and on my phone?

Yes. Install it like an app on a phone or a computer. The shoot day view is built for a phone on set, and your work is on the device, so it keeps working without a signal.

What happens when the year ends?

A plan runs for a year from the day you buy it. Its terms are shown on the plans page and at checkout, before you pay. Your price is locked while you stay, and cancelling takes two taps.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final, with three exceptions that are not discretionary: a duplicate or erroneous charge, a payment for a plan that was never activated on your account, and any refund the law of India requires. Read the Refund Policy

Do I pay in rupees or dollars?

In India you pay in rupees. Elsewhere, prices are shown in US dollars where they are available, and the rupee amount Razorpay charges is always shown before you pay.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. Each stage above shows the tool working on the sample feature, and the plans page compares every feature. While trials are open, the page after you sign in offers one before you buy.
