Four questions to ask before you sign in

login

Why sign in with Google if my work is local?

Sign-in is the door: it tells the studio who you are, so it can check your plan before it opens. Once you are in, your projects also sync to the studio's cloud to reach your other devices. You can switch sync off in Settings, and the copy in your browser stays either way.

cloud_off

Why does offline stop after 14 days?

It works on set without a signal for up to 14 days after you last signed in on that device. After that it needs a connection once, to check your sign-in and your plan again. Nothing you wrote is deleted while it waits.

timer

storage

What leaves my browser, and when?

Your projects, when you are signed in and sync is on. A backup, when you connect your own Google Drive. The text you give an AI tool, when you run one with your own key, to the provider you chose. The studio also talks to its server to sign you in, check your plan and take a payment through Razorpay. While it is open and you are signed in, the device checks in about every 30 seconds so your account stays on one device at a time, and the server sees that device's IP address and browser type. Signed in, it also sends daily counts of which tools are opened and the reason code when a save is refused, never anything you wrote; you can switch both off in Settings, under Privacy. Visit counts on the public pages are daily totals with no name on them. The privacy policy lists every record in full.
