    Films and filmmakers

        Questions

        Before you ask

        I have never made a film. Is this actually for me?

        It is built for exactly that. The {{fms:short.steps}}-step short blueprint assumes you know nothing and explains each step before it asks you for anything — what a logline is and why twenty-five words, what a beat is, why a short has five of them and a feature has more. The {{fms:glossary.count}} terms in the library are there for the moment somebody says “we’ll pick it up in the edit” and you need to know what that means.

        Do I need a camera, a crew or money to start?

        No. Everything on this page happens before a camera is switched on: the idea, the logline, the character, the structure, the scenes and the script. When you do get to the shoot, the studio tells you what a day of it would cost at Chennai rates, which is usually the moment the film becomes a plan instead of a wish.

        Do I need to know screenwriting software?

        No, and you will not have to learn any. The editor formats as you type: start a line with INT. and it is a slug line, type a name in caps and the next line is dialogue. Nothing to configure, no template to download, and the page count is the industry one. If you already have a script, import a .fountain, .fdx or PDF.

        What happens in the first 30 minutes?

        The studio opens on the Dragon sample — a complete feature with its script, schedule, budget and call sheets — and you can open and change every part of it, plus the whole learning library, with no card and nothing installed. It is a half hour to find out whether this is the tool you want before any money is discussed. If somebody gave you an invite code, it is worth seven days instead.

        I am a student. Is this useful for a college film?

        That is the commonest first film there is, and the paperwork is the part nobody teaches. A short blueprint you can hand to a guide, a schedule and a call sheet your unit can read, and a budget at real rates will get you further with a department than a good idea will.

        Can I write in Tamil?

        Yes. Dialogue in Tamil script or in Tanglish, with slug lines and action in English — the arrangement most Tamil units shoot from. Tamil throughout is offered too, with the page count labelled an estimate.

        Does it really work offline, and what if I clear my browser?

        It installs as a web app and every page opens from the device with no connection; your writing is saved to the browser as you type. The other side of that is real: work that was only in that browser goes when the browser is cleared. Export a backup file now and then, or connect Google Drive once and forget about it; signed-in films come back from the cloud on the next sign-in.

        Where is my data, and who can read it?

        On your device, in this browser’s storage, unless you choose otherwise. Your script stays on your machine. Sign in with Google and your films can also sync to the studio’s cloud, where row-level security keeps each account’s rows to itself; connect Google Drive and a backup file sits in your own Drive. Export the whole studio as one file whenever you like. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is collected and why.

        Can I upgrade later?

        Yes. Pay the difference between your plan and the higher one and you are upgraded; nothing you made is touched.

        What if I want my money back?

        Purchases are final. The exceptions are refunded in full to the card, UPI, net banking or wallet you paid with: a duplicate or mistaken charge, a payment for a plan that was never delivered (or we activate it, your choice), and any refund Indian law requires. The Refund & Cancellation Policy has the detail and the address to write to.
