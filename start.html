Built for the real world

Regional presets and offline, on the set

Independent cinema is not always on good Wi-Fi. The studio installs like an app, keeps your work on your device, and carries the paperwork conventions of a Tamil Nadu unit.

● Works offline

Install it from the browser and keep writing, scheduling and printing when the signal drops. Your work is saved on your device first.

● Tamil, Tanglish and CBFC flags

Dialogue in Tamil script or Tanglish with slug lines in English, and CBFC content flags that carry their sources and say when they were last checked.

● FEFSI bata and Chennai rates

A bata table and Chennai rates that feed the budget, each figure dated for when it was last checked, never inflated to look current.

      Frequently asked questions

      How is my screenplay kept private?

      Your scripts, characters and breakdowns are saved in this browser’s own storage on your device. Sign in with Google and your films can also sync to the studio’s cloud, where row-level security keeps each account’s rows to itself; connect Google Drive and a backup file sits in your own Drive. Both are optional. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is collected and why.

      Can I import from Final Draft or Fountain?

      Yes. Bring in a Fountain, Final Draft (.fdx), text or PDF screenplay, and the studio reads the scene headings, action, characters and dialogue into its own format. Export is a properly formatted screenplay PDF. Scanned or encrypted PDFs are refused with a reason rather than guessed at.

      Does it work offline on a film set?

      Yes. The studio installs like an app and caches itself, so you can keep writing, reordering the stripboard and printing call sheets without a signal. Anything you changed offline is still there when you reconnect.

      Are Indian cinema conventions supported?

      Yes. Tamil or Tanglish dialogue with slug lines and action in English, a FEFSI bata table that feeds the budget, CBFC content flags, songs planned as their own blocks of shooting, and Chennai rates that say when they were last checked.

      What do I get in the free trial?

      Every module in the studio, on the Dragon and The Dark Knight sample films, for a limited time — no card and nothing to install. When it ends you choose a plan, and everything you changed is still there.

      Which plan is right for me?

      {{fms:plan.starter}} if you are writing: story, screenplay, drafts and the learning library. {{fms:plan.indie}} if you are going to shoot it: breakdown, stripboard, budget, locations and the paperwork a unit needs. {{fms:plan.pro}} for the whole film, including the shoot day, call sheets, the edit log, deliverables and the AI tools. You can move up later by paying the difference.

      Is it really one payment? No subscription?

      Yes. A plan is bought once with full-time access, and it never renews or lapses. Prices are in Indian rupees, paid through Razorpay, and confirmed at checkout.

      I have never made a film. Is this actually for me?

      It is built for exactly that. The {{fms:short.steps}}-step short blueprint assumes you know nothing and explains each step before it asks you for anything, and the {{fms:glossary.count}} terms in the library are there for the moment somebody says “we’ll pick it up in the edit” and you need to know what that means.

      What if I want my money back?

      You are covered, in full, to the card, UPI, net banking or wallet you paid with, if you were charged twice or by mistake, if you paid and the plan was never delivered (or we activate it, your choice), or wherever Indian law requires a refund. Otherwise a plan is final, which is why the free trial comes first — and on any plan you can export all your work whenever you like. The Refund & Cancellation Policy has the detail and the address to write to.
