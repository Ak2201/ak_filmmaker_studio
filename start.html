Three jobs

What it does

Write the script

A screenplay editor that formats as you type — slug lines, action, cues, dialogue, dual dialogue, transitions — and keeps the page count honest. Dialogue in Tamil or Tanglish with slugs and action in English, which is what the crew shoots from. Import a .fountain, .fdx or PDF; export a properly paginated screenplay PDF.

Screenplay →

Plan the shoot

Every scene heading in the script becomes a scene row. Break it down into cast, props, vehicles and sound; lay the strips out into days; see the day-out-of-days, the locations and a budget at Chennai rates; keep the unit list and print the call sheets — all from one scene model, so nothing is typed twice.

Script breakdown →

Run the set

The shoot day on a phone: today’s scenes, who is called, and what you actually got — shot, part, or dropped. The edit log reads those marks to say what is in the can and what is still owed, and the deliverables list carries you to the censor copy, the DCP and the festival formats.

Five stages

From the idea to the screening

The studio is arranged the way a film is made, in five stages. Each stage is a set of modules, and every module reads the same film — the script, the scenes, the people, the days — so a change in one place shows up in all of them.

      Questions

      Before you ask

      Does it really work offline?

      Yes. The studio installs as a web app, and after your first visit every page opens from the device with no connection. Your writing is saved to the browser as you type. Sync and backup run when you are back online, if you have turned them on; nothing waits for them.

      Where is my data, and who can read it?

      On your device, in this browser’s storage, unless you choose otherwise. Sign in with Google and your films also sync to the studio’s cloud, where row-level security keeps each account’s rows to itself. Connect Google Drive and a backup file sits in your own Drive, with Drive’s own version history. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is collected and why.

      What happens if I clear my browser?

      Work that was only in that browser goes with it — that is what local-first means, and it is why the studio nags you about backups. Export a backup file now and then, or connect Drive once and forget about it; signed-in films come back from the cloud on the next sign-in.

      Can I write in Tamil?

      Yes. Dialogue in Tamil script or in Tanglish, with slug lines and action in English — the arrangement most Tamil units shoot from, and the one where the page count stays arithmetic. Tamil throughout is offered too, with the page count labelled an estimate rather than a number the Courier grid cannot stand behind.

      Why is it invite-only?

      The studio is opening in small groups so that every new member gets looked after. Sign in with Google and ask for an invite, enter a code if somebody gave you one, or buy a plan and come straight in.

      What if I want my money back?

      Purchases are final, which is why the whole studio can be tried free with the sample first. The exceptions are refunded in full to the card, UPI, net banking or wallet you paid with: a duplicate or mistaken charge, a payment for a plan that was never activated (or we activate it, your choice), and any refund Indian law requires. The Refund & Cancellation Policy has the detail and the address to write to.
